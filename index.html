A Note for Santa and the Elves

Dear Santa,

If the North Pole workshop happens to have a little extra magic to spare this season, there is one main item on my wish list this year. I know technology is a bit outside the standard toy-making expertise of the elves, so Apple Store gift cards/credit toward a new phone would be deeply appreciated!

🌨️ My “Red Rider BB gun”: 🌨️

iPhone 18 Pro Max on Apple.com

📱 Why the Upgrade?

My current phone has served me faithfully for four years, but its storage is completely overflowing! It was already bursting at the seams with photos of Callahan, and now with Ellie here, I need all the digital elbow room I can get to capture every single moment.

📏 A Small Lesson in Size

When I chose my current phone four years ago, I went with the smaller frame to test it out. Experience has taught me that the Max size is a much better fit for my hands, so I am hoping to make the jump back up to the larger display this time around.

🎁 How to Help

Because a modern phone is quite an investment, please do not feel any pressure to cover the whole thing! Contributions in the form of Apple Store credit or gift cards toward the fund are the absolute best way to help me reach this goal.

Thank you so much, and Merry Christmas! (Don't forget to double-check for open fires in the fireplace before coming down the chimney!) 🎅✨
